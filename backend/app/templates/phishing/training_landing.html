Pause. Check. Report.

  1. Check the full sender address and reply-to domain.
  2. Be cautious of urgency, secrecy, payment changes, and unexpected attachments.
  3. Open known services through a bookmark or manually entered address.
  4. Never share a password or one-time authentication code.
  5. Report suspicious messages through the approved security channel.
